Introduction

Seeing is only the beginning.

A useful scene model needs more than recognizable objects. It must connect geometry with meaning, keep track of what can change, and support the decisions an agent makes next.

This survey follows that connection: from observations to persistent scene representations, from generated environments to interaction. The chapters compare methods, show their outputs, and examine what their benchmarks actually establish.
