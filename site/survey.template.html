{{t:Why bring these areas together?}}

{{t:A room scan, a rendered scene, and a robot’s map may describe the same place, but they preserve different information. A renderer needs appearance; a planner needs free space; a manipulation policy needs the shape and state of the object it will touch.}}

{{t:We follow those differences through the literature. Each chapter explains a set of methods, uses original figures and videos to show how they work, and connects their results to the tasks they are evaluated on.}}
