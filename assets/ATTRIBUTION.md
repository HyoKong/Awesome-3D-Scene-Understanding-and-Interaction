# Figure attribution

The survey teaser uses a simulation frame from **RoboCasa, Nasiriany et al.**, extracted at 2.0 seconds from the project's [official video](https://robocasa.ai/assets/videos/robot_grid/media16.mp4).

RoboCasa assets and datasets are provided under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as stated in the [official repository](https://github.com/robocasa/robocasa#license).

The source frame is stored in [teaser-source/robocasa-kitchen-robot.png](teaser-source/robocasa-kitchen-robot.png). The composed teaser adds labels, outlines, a schematic hinge, and a reach arrow. These annotations illustrate the survey's scope; they are not model predictions or recorded robot actions.
